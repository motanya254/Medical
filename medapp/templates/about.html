About Dagoretti South Health Facility

Making healthcare easier and convenient to access for Dagoretti South residents

The Dagoretti South Health Facility Finder is a simple, user-friendly tool created to help residents locate the nearest hospitals, clinics, dispensaries and maternity centers quickly and accurately. Many people struggle to find reliable facility information, especially in emergencies. This app solves the problem by centralizing verified health facility data into one clear, easy-to-navigate space.

{# mission and vision #}

{# vision box #}

Vision

{# mission box #}

Mission

Our mission is to bring health access closer to the community, support public awareness, and empower residents of Dagoretti South to connect with the right facility at the right time.
